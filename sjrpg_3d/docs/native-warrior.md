# Native warrior presentation

NativeWarrior3D replaces the warrior hero card; the existing imported skeletal mage stays separate. The reproducible source constructor creates closed-volume boots, greaves, legs, leather torso, shaped steel breastplate, shoulder plates, articulated arms/gauntlets, closed cloth cape, adult face/nose/eyebrows/hair/beard and actual sword blade/guard/grip. Y-up metres, +Z front and sole Y=0 are baked into mesh vertices, with identity root scale. Head top is about1.815m. Six restrained shared material resources distinguish muted steel, leather, forest cloth, skin, hair and bronze; no generated texture or imported sculpt is claimed.

Hips and shoulders are actual Node3D joints. Movement uses Player._run_distance; attacks use _attack_time/_attack_duration; roll and death observe original state. Contact stop and game pause freeze transforms, and reduce-motion attenuates the walk swing. No new movement, attack, damage or animation clock is added. Original sprite recoil and spatial actor_foot still position the root. Original flash intensity drives the material overlay. A dead hero lies down and hides the held sword. This is articulated procedural animation, not a skeletal imported animation clip.

The hero is a single actor with fewer than40 mesh parts and a7000-triangle cap. NPCs use their separately surface-batched three-material family. native_warrior tests geometry/bounds/budgets and actual joint transforms for moved distance, attack progress, contact stop, pause and death. Mobile screenshots and hero/NPC material/scale comparison are required before adoption. Physics, attack ranges, equipment/stat calculations, skill cooldowns and save structure are unchanged.
