Includes:

  • Skeleton model (6174 polygons)
  • Axe models (456 polygons x 2)
  • Skeleton texture (2048x2048 px)
  • Axe texture(512x512 px)

13 Animations:

  • Walk
  • Run
  • 3 Idles
  • 3 Different attacks
  • 2 Damage animations
  • 2 Death
  • Jump
