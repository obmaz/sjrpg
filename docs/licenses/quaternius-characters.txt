Quaternius rendered character assets
===================================

Models, textures and source animations by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com).
The four FREE Standard source packs below are CC0 1.0 Universal.
No paid Source/Pro content is used. The project adds original sword/wand
geometry, split robe panels, matte material treatment and the offline bake.
No KayKit or image-generated draft sprites are included in this asset set.
